# Platformers: the movement study

**Research checkpoint: 23 October 2026.** This is a broad, source-grounded discovery study, not a claim to catalogue every platformer ever made or to have personally play-tested every entry. Its purpose is to make a large collection navigable: find the established reference points, understand why different games belong together, then follow a useful route into newer releases and less-obvious discoveries.

The complete, current inventory is generated in the [Platformers hub](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Games/wiki/Platformers). Every accepted record has an exact official identity, source links, an explicit release state, a game dossier and a place in the [media atlas](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Games/wiki/Platformers-Media). The [new and upcoming shelf](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Games/wiki/Platformers-New-and-Upcoming) is derived from the checked snapshot, not an old announcement list. The [gem shortlist](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Games/wiki/Platformers-Gems) is editorial and sample-aware, not a declaration that a popularity threshold objectively defines a hidden gem.

## 1. Start with the kind of movement you actually enjoy

The most useful question is not simply which game has the highest score. It is **what you want to become better at, and how much friction you want between attempts**. A player who loves discovering a route in a large 3D world may dislike repeating a tiny lethal room; a player who loves short precision challenges may find a deliberately opaque exploration map exhausting.

## 2. Precision is a family, not a single difficulty slider

[Celeste](https://store.steampowered.com/app/504230/), [Super Meat Boy](https://store.steampowered.com/app/40800/) and [N++](https://store.steampowered.com/app/230270/) are valuable reference points precisely because their movement contracts differ. A dash-and-climb toolkit, an emphasis on committed momentum, and a spare physics-driven room can all produce hard platforming without testing exactly the same skill. Choose according to the feel you want to practice, not the assumption that a higher death count means a better game.

For a less-obvious route, investigate [Dustforce DX](https://store.steampowered.com/app/65300/) for continuous lines through a space, [Octahedron](https://store.steampowered.com/app/434660/) for creating temporary footholds, and [Super Magbot](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1205170/) for movement organized around magnetic forces. These are useful discoveries because they change the central verb. They are not merely familiar jumping with different artwork.

The compact-game branch includes LOVE, LOVE 2: kuso, LOVE 3, Pinkman, Super Cable Boy and Jubilee. The creator-first branch includes the original CELESTE Classic and Celeste Classic 2. Compactness can make a game easier to fit into a week without making its execution easier. The correct comparison is the density and variety of movement decisions, not a guessed hours-per-dollar calculation.

A different edge of the family is the sustained-climb challenge: Jump King, Getting Over It and A Difficult Game About Climbing. Their long consequences for mistakes are a meaningful design distinction from frequent checkpoint restarts. They are included as clearly named mastery routes, not recommended indiscriminately to someone who asks for a relaxing platformer.

**Practical route:** start with one welcoming reference, then change one major rule. After Celeste, try N++ for momentum, Octahedron for manufactured platforms, or Remnants of Naezith for grappling. Buying several similar-looking difficult games at once is less informative than identifying which movement loop makes practice satisfying.

## 3. 3D platforming: authored courses, collectathons and movement playgrounds

The collection distinguishes a tightly authored sequence of courses from a broad space you learn to traverse in your own way. [Psychonauts 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/607080/) is a strong story-and-world route; [Tinykin](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1599020/) emphasizes readable miniature environments and exploration. Neither should be treated as a substitute for a pure high-intensity precision game just because both involve jumping.

For an independent 3D discovery route, begin with [Lunistice](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1701800/), [Corn Kidz 64](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2575900/) and [Cavern of Dreams](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2059660/). Their smaller scale and distinct movement personalities make them useful alternatives to the large commercial mascots. Then investigate [Pseudoregalia](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2365810/) when the attraction is learning how new movement options reinterpret the same architecture.

[Yellow Taxi Goes Vroom](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2011780/) deliberately makes vehicle handling carry the traversal. [Penny's Big Breakaway](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Games/wiki/Platformers) and the Demon Turf/Demon Tides lineage are worth following for expressive movement. These should be sampled for control feel rather than assumed to share a familiar double-jump-and-collect contract.

The current [Big Hops Together](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1221480/) listing describes tongue interactions, climbing, player-created routes and added cooperative play. The study preserves app ID 1221480 across the name change from Big Hops; a rename is not a newly discovered sequel. Its checked Steam release date is 12 January 2026, and a soundtrack or an inconsistent secondary page is not allowed to replace that game's date.

For a gentler adjacent route, use A Short Hike, Lil Gator Game, Smushi Come Home and Gecko Gods. These are labelled exploration adventures or traversal hybrids where appropriate. Including them expands useful discovery without pretending that calm climbing, gliding and environmental puzzles are the same experience as Crash Bandicoot's authored obstacle courses.

**What matters before buying:** camera behaviour, input remapping, the penalty for a missed jump, optional completionist demands, and whether exploration is signposted or deliberately uncertain. Attractive cartoon art does not answer any of those questions by itself.

## 4. Speed and expressive movement: the route becomes the reward

[Pizza Tower](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2231450/) and [ANTONBLAST](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1887400/) sit on a lively momentum-and-destruction branch. Sonic Mania, Freedom Planet 2 and Spark the Electric Jester 3 offer other routes through fast traversal and character-specific action. The study groups them by a useful player interest while keeping their individual identities and cautions separate.

First-person routes are explicitly marked. [Neon White](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1533420/) ties route choices to expendable weapon-card abilities; [Cyber Hook](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1130410/) focuses attention on grappling lines. Ghostrunner adds lethal combat pressure. These are not automatically good recommendations for someone who dislikes first-person cameras or combat, even when their traversal is central.

[SANABI](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1562700/) is a useful route when grappling and narrative momentum are both attractive. Katana ZERO is included as an action-platformer boundary case, where solving a short encounter is as important as crossing a gap. Haste, Denshattack! and Seafrog broaden the movement vocabulary further; their run, vehicle or trick-action structures remain visible rather than being hidden inside a generic platformer label.

**Practical route:** choose a single movement interest, such as swinging, air control, maintaining momentum or optimizing a timed stage. Use the official footage and the dossier's cautions to find a game built around that interest. A review percentage cannot tell you whether you will enjoy a specific camera perspective or control constraint.

## 5. Puzzle-platformers and cinematic journeys

The puzzle branch includes [Braid, Anniversary Edition](https://store.steampowered.com/app/499180/), FEZ, The Swapper, The Pedestrian, ElecHead and Toodee and Topdee. These reward reasoning about time, perspective, connectivity or a simple systemic rule. Reflex skill can help, but it is not always the main gate.

The cinematic branch includes [INSIDE](https://store.steampowered.com/app/304430/), LIMBO, Planet of Lana and its sequel, the Little Nightmares games, REANIMAL and REPLACED. Here, pacing, staging and atmosphere may matter more than movement improvisation. The study does not use short campaign length as a proxy for either poor value or weak design; it instead explains what the experience is trying to provide.

[Webbed](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1390350/) is a particularly useful gem lead because its web construction changes how you interact with the environment. [Yoku's Island Express](https://store.steampowered.com/app/334940/) brings pinball movement into exploration. [Ugly](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1983220/) and INMOST offer darker alternatives. All are better understood by their actual interaction rules than by a thumbnail or a broad adventure tag.

The contemporary physics-puzzle branch includes [Bionic Bay](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1928690/) and [Darwin's Paradox!](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2989180/). They are discovery leads, not a promise that every player will prefer them to older benchmarks. Current reception and sample size remain separate evidence.

**Important boundary:** Portal and Portal 2 are included as first-person spatial-puzzle and traversal hybrids. Their presence does not redefine every puzzle game as a platformer. The individual record states the adjacency so the reader can include or disregard that branch consciously.

## 6. Exploration and Metroidvanias: movement, combat and navigation are separate preferences

The core references include Hollow Knight, Ori, ANIMAL WELL, Prince of Persia The Lost Crown, the Axiom Verge games and Bloodstained. They provide different balances of navigation, combat, secrets and traversal. Someone who loves one balance should not automatically be pushed toward the hardest example of another.

A movement-oriented route runs through [Lone Fungus](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1674780/), [Rusted Moss](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1772830/) and [Bo: Path of the Teal Lotus](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1614440/). An exploration-and-discovery route can lead to [Environmental Station Alpha](https://store.steampowered.com/app/350070/), Astalon, Haiku, the Robot, Islets and Alwa's Legacy. These are useful less-obvious investigations because they offer a specific reason to explore beyond the most familiar names.

For combat-forward interests, the study also covers Nine Sols, Blasphemous, GRIME, Death's Gambit: Afterlife and related entries. Their presence is not a claim that difficult combat is the defining virtue of the whole genre. The relevant question is how much of the experience you want to spend fighting versus navigating, experimenting and moving.

Unusual hybrids are explicit. Crypt Custodian is top-down. Rain World is an ecosystem-survival platformer rather than a conventional ability-gated exploration game. Laika uses motorcycle traversal. RUBATO has an eccentric physics-led approach. These distinctions are more useful than inflating a list by silently treating every adjacent action game as interchangeable.

The latest shelf is checked against the current official store state. [MIO: Memories in Orbit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1672810/) is a January 2026 release; [Well Dweller](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3699590/) is a September 2026 release. Older previews are not evidence that either remains unreleased. Conversely, an announced date is not proof that a still-upcoming title can be bought and played today.

## 7. Authored action, roguelite runs and cooperative play

The authored-action route includes Shovel Knight, The Messenger, [Gravity Circuit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/858710/), Cyber Shadow, Kaze and the Wild Masks, the Mega Man collections, Gunvolt and the Grapple Dog games. Classic collections are counted as the exact product represented, not multiplied into several fictional new releases. Run-and-gun games such as HUNTDOWN, Blazing Chrome and Iron Meat have an explicit combat-forward scope.

Recent action-platformer leads include NINJA GAIDEN: Ragebound, SHINOBI: Art of Vengeance and Windswept. The important exercise is to compare the exact release's movement, encounter pacing and reception rather than buying or dismissing it solely on franchise reputation. Cute presentation is not a difficulty rating: the Windswept creator specifically presents a demanding journey.

The roguelite branch shares stable identities with the existing Roguelites hub. Spelunky and Spelunky 2 emphasize knowledge and execution under changing conditions; Rogue Legacy 2 adds persistent progression; Dead Cells, Astral Ascent and Spiritfall bring different combat priorities. A failed run, a short checkpoint retry and a persistent-upgrade loop are different commitments. The collection makes room for all three without implying that they suit the same evening or the same player.

Cooperative recommendations likewise need more than a co-op tag. It Takes Two and Split Fiction are routes for authored two-player collaboration; Trine explores complementary abilities; Bread & Fred makes coordination itself a demanding traversal problem. Ultimate Chicken Horse is competitive stage-building chaos, not a gentle shared story. The exact source page remains the authority for local versus online support, player requirements, Friend's Pass conditions and platform restrictions.

The console branch includes [ASTRO BOT](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/astro-bot/), [Super Mario Odyssey](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/super-mario-odyssey-switch/), Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury, Super Mario Bros. Wonder, Super Mario Maker 2, Donkey Kong Country editions, Kirby, Yoshi and Metroid Dread. Hardware requirements are shown directly; the absence of a Steam listing is not treated as absence from the genre.

[Donkey Kong Bananza](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/donkey-kong-bananza-switch-2/) and [Yoshi and the Mysterious Book](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/yoshi-and-the-mysterious-book-switch-2/) require Switch 2. The enhanced Kirby package is distinguished from its separate upgrade. Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 is labelled as a newer package of older games, not two newly created 2025 originals. These edition distinctions prevent a newest-first shelf from becoming misleading.

For free creator releases, start with [CELESTE Classic](https://maddymakesgamesinc.itch.io/celesteclassic), [Celeste Classic 2](https://maddymakesgamesinc.itch.io/celeste-classic-2), [Celeste 64](https://maddymakesgamesinc.itch.io/celeste64) and [SuperTux](https://www.supertux.org/). The small Celeste anniversary releases are not free copies of the commercial game. SuperTux's current version is not its original release year. ASTRO's Playroom is free for the relevant PS5 hardware, not a free PC download.

This pass does not claim a complete emulation, ROM-hack, fan-game or historical-console inventory. It does not link unauthorized commercial ROM distributions. Older hardware-only classics and creator projects without a sufficiently clear current identity remain future research areas rather than invented retail entries.

## 9. How to read the evidence

**Identity comes first.** A guessed app ID is only a discovery hint. Accepted Steam entries must match an exact normalized title or a manually reviewed current-title alias, and must be games rather than soundtracks, DLC or substituted demos. Ambiguous candidates are retained in the reconciliation ledger. Big Hops Together, LOVE 2: kuso and Lone Fungus: Melody of Spores illustrate why titles can change while stable IDs should not.

**Editorial fit is not the review percentage.** Fit describes how strongly the game belongs in a route. The existing library caps recommendations when reception is weak or the sample is tiny. Upcoming records have no purchase score. A 100% positive sample of a handful of reviews is not treated as stronger evidence than a large, mature sample. Console games do not receive fabricated Steam ratings, and native itch.io ratings retain their own scale.

**A gem is a discovery lead, not a scientific category.** The generated shortlist starts from editorial candidates and applies a visible reception/sample rule: at least 30 and at most 5,000 checked Steam reviews, at least 80% positive, sufficient editorial fit, no review-refresh error and not upcoming. This is a useful filter, not a measurement of total worldwide audience or artistic quality. Games outside that window can still be excellent less-obvious recommendations in the study.

**Dates have scopes.** Steam dates describe the exact store edition. Remasters, collections and later ports can be recent purchases without being new original designs. Update dates are not launch dates. Prices are dated US-store snapshots, not a live offer or a worldwide availability promise. A verified page without current purchase evidence is labelled accordingly.

**Media has provenance and limits.** Steam artwork, screenshots and returned videos retain exact source links. Console and creator artwork is taken only from the reviewed page, not from a search result for a similarly named game. Missing streams remain visible gaps; the official page is still linked. A returned video URL is not the same claim as a successful playback test. Browser QA records what was actually opened, decoded and played.

**Breadth and depth are not conflated.** Individual editorial notes are labelled separately from general subgenre study lenses. The full catalogue offers broad factual discovery; the individually written comparisons provide a deeper route through it. No part of this study claims personal completion, personal playtime or firsthand hardware testing.

## 10. Keep it a living collection

The source ledger, dated provider snapshots, reviewed aliases and editorial notes are separate files. Refreshing metadata must not erase the editorial work, duplicate a renamed game, replace a console edition, or reset the user's private notes and favorites. Cross-genre titles appear in multiple hubs but remain one stable record in the complete catalogue.

The new hub uses the existing search, filters, card/table/gallery views, comparisons, notes, ratings, backup/import and export controls. Every record remains available in the dataset and DOM. The generated wiki, illustrated atlas, individual dossiers, CSV index and portable HTML are different views of that same canonical snapshot, not disconnected lists that drift independently.

Use the [updating guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Games/blob/main/docs/UPDATING.md) for the refresh and verification commands. The generated coverage report records accepted identities, unresolved candidates, editorial-note depth and media gaps. Native wiki, release and Pages publication must be checked separately from a successful local build.
