# Signs of Life

![Signs of Life official artwork](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/263200/header.jpg?t=1787930581)

**[Open official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/263200/)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A263200)** · [Roguelites](Roguelites)

Signs of Life is a sci-fi survival sandbox platformer with a combination of procedurally generated and hand-crafted content. You'll explore mysterious locations, mine blocks for resources, and meet strange (and some familiar) creatures, some of which don't even want to kill you and eat your corpse.
