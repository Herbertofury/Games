## Field notes

### [Dead Cells](Game-588650-Dead-Cells)

A high-priority action-roguelite crossover where traversal and combat execution work inside repeatable runs.

**Caution:** Combat and build decisions are central. Separate the base game, expansions and bundles when comparing editions.

**Scope:** Roguelite platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/588650/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A588650)

### [Spelunky 2](Game-418530-Spelunky-2)

A central procedural-platforming reference: movement judgment, systemic interactions and risk management make knowledge valuable even when a run ends.

**Caution:** Run failure is a substantial part of the design; it is not a forgiving level-by-level checkpoint adventure.

**Scope:** Roguelite platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/418530/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A418530)
