Godsworn is a classic RTS with single-player & co-op focus. Experience an epic fantasy retelling of the Baltic Crusades set in a world inspired by pagan mythology. Choose a divine hero, rally worshippers alongside mythical creatures, and smite the unworthy!

## Why it is here

A particularly promising fantasy RTS for players who want heroes and base building without abandoning classic strategy structure. Still Early Access, despite strong reception.
