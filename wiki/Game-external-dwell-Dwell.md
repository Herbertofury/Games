# Dwell

![Dwell official artwork](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/48/47592/h1.jpg)

**[Open official page](https://www.moddb.com/mods/dwell)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Adwell)** · [Boomer shooters](Boomer-Shooters)

A sprawling Quake conversion with ancient architecture, a reworked arsenal, unusual monsters, and exploration-heavy campaigns.

## Why it is here

One of the richer Quake-world discoveries. The released episodes are substantial, but the planned four-episode project is not presented as complete.
