# Mayhem Brawler

![Mayhem Brawler official artwork](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1306720/header.jpg?t=1743162376)

**[Open official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1306720/)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A1306720)** · [Roguelites](Roguelites)

Mayhem Brawler is an urban fantasy themed beat 'em up that brings back the 90s arcade vibe to present day. With comic book style art and kick-ass soundtracks, it offers a unique experience that you can tackle solo or as co-op with friends while defining the next step in the story with your choices.
