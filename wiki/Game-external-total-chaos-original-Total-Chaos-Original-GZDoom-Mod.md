### Before you play

Original free GZDoom Director's Cut 1.40, not the separate Unreal Engine remake. The official package includes GZDoom and does not require Total Chaos 1.0 or DOOM2.WAD.

### Latest verified update

Original mod Director's Cut 1.40: August 8, 2022. The later commercial remake has a separate release history.
