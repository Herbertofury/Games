## Field notes

### [Celeste](Game-504230-Celeste)

The reference route for learning a small move set deeply: climbing, dashing and short retry loops turn difficult screens into readable problems. Start here for precision with a strong emotional through-line.

**Caution:** Optional late-game challenges are much harder than the opening route; assist settings and self-selected goals matter more than completionist pressure.

**Scope:** Precision platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/504230/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A504230)

### [N++ (NPLUSPLUS)](Game-230270-N-NPLUSPLUS)

Momentum, clean silhouettes and tiny spatial decisions form a remarkably durable precision vocabulary. A good choice for players who enjoy learning the geometry of a room rather than collecting new abilities.

**Caution:** The scale of the level set is an invitation to choose challenges, not a promise that every player should clear everything.

**Scope:** Precision platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/230270/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A230270)

### [Super Meat Boy](Game-40800-Super-Meat-Boy)

A direct, fast-reset approach to momentum and hazardous obstacle courses. Useful beside Celeste because the feel of committing to a jump is different even when both reward repetition.

**Caution:** Demanding execution and frequent deaths are central, not incidental; do not confuse this with the auto-running sequel.

**Scope:** Precision platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/40800/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A40800)

### [VVVVVV](Game-70300-VVVVVV)

Removing the conventional jump and asking you to flip gravity creates a clear demonstration of how one changed rule can sustain a whole platformer.

**Caution:** Minimal presentation does not imply easy execution; hazard timing remains demanding.

**Scope:** Precision platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/70300/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A70300)

### [The End Is Nigh](Game-583470-The-End-Is-Nigh)
