Enshrouded is a solo and co-op survival action RPG where your freedom and creativity come first. Conquer the Shroud, a corrupting fog devouring the kingdom of Embervale: explore a handcrafted world full of secrets, forge your hero your way, and build anything, anywhere in a fully terraformable land.

## Why it is here

A strong choice when building and adventurous exploration matter as much as eating and drinking. Handcrafted fantasy discovery with an unusually expressive construction system.
