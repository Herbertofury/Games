## Field notes

### [Celeste 64: Fragments of the Mountain](Game-external-celeste-64-Celeste-64-Fragments-of-the-Mountain)

A small free 3D experiment that translates Celeste's appetite for movement challenges into a compact anniversary playground. Worth trying for its concentrated traversal ideas rather than expecting a full commercial sequel.

**Caution:** Short standalone anniversary game. A recent page update is not its original release date; controller recommended by the creator. Sources and available builds are on the exact creator page.

**Scope:** Free creator platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://maddymakesgamesinc.itch.io/celeste64) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Aceleste-64)

### [CELESTE Classic](Game-external-celeste-classic-CELESTE-Classic)

A compact browser-playable look at the original mountain-climbing idea. Its small scale makes the movement loop easy to inspect without suggesting it replaces the later commercial Celeste.

**Caution:** PICO-8 original, not a free download of the full 2018 commercial game. Original date is left unspecified here rather than copied from a recent page update.

**Scope:** Free creator platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://maddymakesgamesinc.itch.io/celesteclassic) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Aceleste-classic)

### [Celeste Classic 2: Lani's Trek](Game-external-celeste-classic-2-Celeste-Classic-2-Lani-s-Trek)

A concise grappling-focused variation that demonstrates how changing a movement tool can produce a different platforming language inside a tiny game.

**Caution:** Free PICO-8 anniversary game, not a numbered commercial Celeste sequel. Native host ratings, when available, retain their original five-point scale.

**Scope:** Free creator platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://maddymakesgamesinc.itch.io/celeste-classic-2) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Aceleste-classic-2)

### [SuperTux](Game-external-supertux-SuperTux)

A free, open-source traditional platformer with a long-lived community and an included route into custom levels. A useful choice for exploring platforming without a store purchase.

**Caution:** Choose a stable build from the official download page; nightly builds and older ports have different compatibility. A new version number is not an original release date.

**Scope:** Free creator platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://www.supertux.org/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Asupertux)
