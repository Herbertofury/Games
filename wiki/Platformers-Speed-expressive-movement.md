## Field notes

### [Pizza Tower](Game-2231450-Pizza-Tower)

A high-priority pick for expressive momentum, escalating stage chaos and replaying routes with much greater confidence than the first pass.

**Caution:** Its frantic presentation and pursuit-driven structure can be overwhelming; strong fit does not mean low intensity.

**Scope:** Momentum and speedrunning · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2231450/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A2231450)

### [Neon White](Game-1533420-Neon-White)

Route discovery and movement decisions are tied to disposable weapon-card abilities. A natural starting point for players who enjoy turning a first clear into a faster, more deliberate run.

**Caution:** First-person shooter and time-trial hybrid, not a conventional side-scrolling platformer. Its story presentation and speedrunning loop appeal for different reasons.

**Scope:** First-person speedrunning shooter / platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1533420/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A1533420)

### [Katana ZERO](Game-460950-Katana-ZERO)

Short lethal encounters, rewinds and deliberate movement make a useful action-platformer boundary case. The appeal is solving a scene stylishly as much as jumping across it.

**Caution:** Violent action and narrative content; not a pure traversal game or a roguelite merely because attempts repeat.

**Scope:** Cinematic action platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/460950/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A460950)

### [Ghostrunner](Game-1139900-Ghostrunner)

First-person traversal and lethal combat create an aggressive execution puzzle. Best considered for players who want positioning, movement and attack timing inseparable.

**Caution:** One-hit lethality and demanding action make this a specialist choice, not a universally welcoming movement game.

**Scope:** First-person combat / parkour platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note

[Exact official source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1139900/) · [Media and details](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A1139900)
