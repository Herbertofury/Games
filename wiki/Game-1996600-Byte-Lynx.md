Byte Lynx is a fast-paced RTS with no units. Build defenses, dismantle your enemy, and steal their buildings in this deeply tactical building combat-based strategy game. Expand your arsenal across a series of diverse levels!

## Why it is here

An inventive tiny RTS built around networked structures and expansion rather than ordinary unit armies. Excellent as a mechanical detour, not the closest match to Zero Hour.
