Eggsecution Squad is a fast, chaotic egg brawler for up to 4 players — local or online. Grab 14+ wild weapons, dash, block and punch across 5 maps, and knock the other eggs off or kill them. Last egg standing wins. Dress your egg up and beat up your friends (in game).
