Cat Powered UFO is a blend of turn-based RPG combat and old-school Zelda top-down action. Collect cats, visit crazy dimensions, and be a jerk to NPCs. Find combos of "Illegal bonuses" to break the combat system! Cat Powered UFO is a quirky and light-hearted game with a self-aware sense of humor.
