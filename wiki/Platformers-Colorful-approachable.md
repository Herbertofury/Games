## Field notes

### [Super Mario Bros. Wonder](Game-external-super-mario-bros-wonder-Super-Mario-Bros-Wonder)

A strong modern 2D starting point where level transformations interrupt expectations and badges change the movement vocabulary. Optional challenges leave room to grow beyond the welcoming first impression.

**Caution:** Local cooperative play and online features are not identical. This record is the original Switch game; consult the exact edition before buying any later upgrade or package.
