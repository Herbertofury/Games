# Zero Hour and C&C: the mod command center

A free mod download does not mean its required commercial base game is free. Generals Evolution requires **Red Alert 3**, while Contra, Rise of the Reds, ShockWave and The End of Days require **Zero Hour**. OpenRA-based standalones are labelled separately.

## [C&C: ShockWave](Game-external-cc-shockwave-C-C-ShockWave)

![C&C: ShockWave](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/7/6301/woot.jpg)

The most natural more-of-Zero-Hour recommendation: more distinctive generals, new arsenals, and familiar base building without discarding the original game's character.

**Requirements:** Requires Command & Conquer: Generals - Zero Hour.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/cc-shockwave)

## [Command & Conquer: Combined Arms](Game-external-combined-arms-Command-Conquer-Combined-Arms)

![Command & Conquer: Combined Arms](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/34/33634/title.png)

A modernized C&C crossover with mixed-era armies, campaigns, and skirmish depth. Particularly good when you want more C&C rather than a distant genre cousin.

**Requirements:** Standalone OpenRA-based game; the original commercial C&C games are not required.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/command-conquer-combined-arms)

## [Command & Conquer: Generals Evolution](Game-external-generals-evolution-Command-Conquer-Generals-Evolution)

![Command & Conquer: Generals Evolution](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/22/21235/ModdbButton.1.png)

Generals-style armies rebuilt in Red Alert 3's engine. A visual and mechanical reinterpretation rather than a native Zero Hour patch.

**Requirements:** Requires Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3, NOT Generals or Zero Hour.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/command-and-conquer-generals-evolution)

## [Contra X](Game-external-contra-x-Contra-X)

![Contra X](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/11/10422/Install_Final_Contra_ModDB-bg.1.jpg)

Keep the Zero Hour foundation and turn the faction identities, challenge and powerful arsenals up dramatically. A direct extension of the style you asked for.

**Requirements:** Requires Command & Conquer: Generals - Zero Hour. Use the base-game and patch requirements specified for the exact Contra release; a standalone mod package is not a free base-game license.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/contra)

## [Mental Omega](Game-external-mental-omega-Mental-Omega)

![Mental Omega](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/5/4016/mo-moddb-thumbz.png)

A huge Red Alert 2 expansion with deep faction asymmetry and a sprawling alternate campaign. One of the best ways to make a classic C&C feel enormous again.

**Requirements:** Requires Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2 and Yuri's Revenge.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/mental-omega)

## [Rise of the Reds](Game-external-rise-of-the-reds-Rise-of-the-Reds)

![Rise of the Reds](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/5/4333/Untitled-1.jpg)

Adds Russia and the European Continental Alliance while rebuilding the original armies. A major upgrade for asymmetrical Zero Hour skirmishes.

**Requirements:** Requires Command & Conquer: Generals - Zero Hour.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/rise-of-the-reds)

## [The Dawn of the Tiberium Age](Game-external-dawn-tiberium-age-The-Dawn-of-the-Tiberium-Age)

![The Dawn of the Tiberium Age](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/11/10865/Mod_DB_DTA_logo.png)

Brings the first C&C and Red Alert armies together with a substantial single-player and co-op mission library. A particularly strong free skirmish-and-campaign choice.

**Requirements:** Standalone; the original Tiberian Sun game is not required.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/the-dawn-of-the-tiberium-age)

## [The End of Days](Game-external-end-of-days-zh-The-End-of-Days)

![The End of Days](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/12/11840/TEOD_logo.png)

A modern-military reinterpretation of Zero Hour with Russia and expanded faction specializations. More grounded equipment than the original's most fantastical weapons.

**Requirements:** Requires Command & Conquer: Generals - Zero Hour. This is the RTS mod, not the unrelated survival game with the same title.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/the-end-of-days)

## [Twisted Insurrection](Game-external-twisted-insurrection-Twisted-Insurrection)

![Twisted Insurrection](https://media.moddb.com/images/mods/1/11/10974/TIPromoBox.png)

An alternative-history Tiberian Sun world with rebuilt factions, a large campaign, and excellent atmosphere. Best for a fresh C&C setting.

**Requirements:** Standalone Tiberian Sun total conversion.

[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/twisted-insurrection)
