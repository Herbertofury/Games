# Project Osiris

**[Open official page](https://arcturusdeluxe.itch.io/project-osiris)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Aproject-osiris)** · [Boomer shooters](Boomer-Shooters)

A free remake of Alien Breed 3D in GZDoom, rebuilding its 16-level campaign with modern controls and lighting.

## Why it is here

An excellent deep cut for chunky sci-fi environments and deliberate exploration, particularly if you missed the Amiga original.
