As one of the last human survivors, you find yourself stranded on an uninhabited island. With a few other victims of the crash, can you craft, mine, and build your way to survival?

## Why it is here

A compact-looking game that grows into a surprisingly broad mining, crafting and exploration adventure. Good when you want clear progression rather than a completely directionless sandbox.
