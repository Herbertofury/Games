# Volcanoids

![Volcanoids official artwork](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/951440/header.jpg?t=1733747150)

**[Open official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/951440/)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A951440)** · [Roguelites](Roguelites)

A base-building open-world survival shooter that replaces your typical base with a giant drill. Alone or with friends, explore an island plagued by eruptions, upgrade your drill, and fight robots to uncover what's been hiding beneath the surface.

## Why it is here

A mobile drilling machine gives the usual survival base a purpose and a personality. Explore, manufacture and retreat underground instead of defending a static cabin.
