Cossacks 3 is the return of RTS classics. Obtain resources, build cities and research technologies while taking command of great armies, crushing enemies in grand historical warfares on land and sea!

## Why it is here

A historical mass-army choice for large infantry formations and economy building. Much closer to the giant-battle fantasy than to Zero Hour's specialized modern units.
