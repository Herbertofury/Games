# Ashes: Blackwater

![Ashes: Blackwater official artwork](https://media.moddb.com/images/downloads/1/308/307690/Cover.png)

**[Open official page](https://www.moddb.com/mods/ashes-2063/downloads/ashes-blackwater)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Aashes-blackwater)** · [Boomer shooters](Boomer-Shooters)

An alternate-timeline Ashes add-on that reconnects six existing Afterglow and Hard Reset maps into a tougher hub-based adventure.

## Why it is here

A genuinely recent reason to revisit Ashes. Expect remixed spaces, new dialogue, and a demanding night in Atlanta, not six wholly new maps.

### Before you play

The June 20, 2026 v1.5 download is packaged as a standalone. Extract it and use its included launcher .bat. Hardcore mode requires the supplied GZDoom/config and its launcher. Earlier releases used a different setup.

### Latest verified update

Version 1.5, updated June 20, 2026, adopts a standalone package. Six remixed Afterglow/Hard Reset maps form a hub, rather than six newly made maps.
