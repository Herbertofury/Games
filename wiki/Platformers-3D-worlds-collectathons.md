A priority route for players who want authored 3D spaces to express character and story rather than functioning only as obstacle courses.

**Caution:** Narrative and exploration are major attractions; not the best substitute for a purely technical precision game.

**Scope:** 3D exploration platformer · **Editorial depth:** Individual editorial note
