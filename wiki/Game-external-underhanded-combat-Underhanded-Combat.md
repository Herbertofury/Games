# Underhanded Combat

![Underhanded Combat official artwork](https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzIxOTk1NDExLmpwZw==/original/Pk10Zb.jpg)

**[Open official page](https://nathanjoy.itch.io/underhanded-combat)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=external%3Aunderhanded-combat)** · [Roguelites](Roguelites)

THE GAME Underhanded combat is a local 2 player platform shooter with samurai gunn inspired movement where you battle within an japanese inspired dojo to ultimatly take out the other ninja. LORE 2 ninja's find themselves in a war between 2 different ninja factions. to determine which comes out on top they fight eachother through dirty means. you sneak into the enemy's dojo and must defeat the other ninja before backup arives on sundown.
