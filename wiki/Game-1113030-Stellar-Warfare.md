# Stellar Warfare

**[Open official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1113030/)** · **[Watch trailers and open gallery](https://herbertofury.github.io/Games/#game=steam%3A1113030)** · [Real-time strategy](Real-Time-Strategy)

Space RTS with base building and ship customization. Personal passion project. More than 200 available ships, weapons and modules to design your fleet with. Contains a small campaign, several single and multiplayer game modes and a coop game mode. Also contains Wave defense!

## Why it is here

An indie fleet RTS with space-base construction and modular fleets. A promising niche choice, but still Early Access and smaller in audience than Homeworld or Sins.
