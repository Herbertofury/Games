# Creator support and Patreon

**A support link is not a game license.** Public builds, demos, paid releases, and membership benefits are distinct. The library does not claim to have inspected member-only posts or verified private downloads. Read current creator tier descriptions before subscribing.

## Selaco

A detailed tactical-combat and exploration pick built on GZDoom. Its Early Access status remains explicit, and creator support is separate from the game purchase.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1592280/) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/PlaySelaco)

## Mohrta

A particularly interesting dark-fantasy exploration FPS for your taste in atmospheric worlds and unusual weapons.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2881610/) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/scumhead) · [GOG store](https://www.gog.com/en/game/mohrta)

## Doom: The Golden Souls 2

One of the most distinctive free mod recommendations: an entire adventure rather than only a new weapon set.

[itch.io download](https://batandy.itch.io/goldensouls2) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/Batandy)

## G String

Slip into the Standard Issue Biosuit as Myo Hyori, a gifted Korean teenage girl who must face the perils of the future. Survive many hazards, explore a seemingly endless metropolis and beyond as the world around you implodes and history marches towards its inevitable conclusion.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1224600/) · [ModDB project](https://www.moddb.com/mods/g-string) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/eyaura) · [ModDB project](https://www.moddb.com/mods/g-string?filter=t&rating=8) · [ModDB project](https://rss.moddb.com/mods/g-string/articles/feed/rss.xml) · [ModDB project](https://rss.moddb.com/mods/g-string/downloads/feed/rss.xml)

## Vomitoreum

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1549750) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/scumhead) · [itch.io store](https://scumhead.itch.io/vomitoreumgame)

Explore a decaying industrial world left behind in humanity's never-ending race to conquer the stars. Beneath its iron skies, desperate souls kill and steal for passage to the still-plentiful frontier worlds. Sink or swim in this high-octane first-person shooter with immersive sim elements.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1692240/) · [GOG store](https://www.gog.com/en/game/fortunes_run) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/TeamFortune)

## Diluvian Ultra

Diluvian Ultra is a single-player, first-person shooter focusing on a retro aesthetic and fast-paced, strategic combat. The game is set in its own expansive fictive universe. Rather than gritty or dark, Diluvian Ultra is colorful and fantastical, like the cover of pulp sci-fi novels of the past.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1306970/) · [GOG store](https://www.gog.com/en/game/diluvian_ultra) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/diluvian) · [Epic Games store](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/diluvian-ultra-a5ad8c)

A very promising-looking old-school RTS with striking industrial factions and detailed pixel artwork. It is an upcoming project, not something this catalog claims you can buy and play today.
