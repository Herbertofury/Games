# Creator support and Patreon

**A support link is not a game license.** Public builds, demos, paid releases, and membership benefits are distinct. The library does not claim to have inspected member-only posts or verified private downloads. Read current creator tier descriptions before subscribing.

## Selaco

A detailed tactical-combat and exploration pick built on GZDoom. Its Early Access status remains explicit, and creator support is separate from the game purchase.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1592280/) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/PlaySelaco)

## Mohrta

A particularly interesting dark-fantasy exploration FPS for your taste in atmospheric worlds and unusual weapons.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2881610/) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/scumhead)

## Doom: The Golden Souls 2

One of the most distinctive free mod recommendations: an entire adventure rather than only a new weapon set.

[itch.io download](https://batandy.itch.io/goldensouls2) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/Batandy)

## Vomitoreum

Vomitoreum is an FPS Metroidvania with a focus on atmosphere and horror, inspired by the paintings of Zdzisław Beksiński.

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1549750/) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/scumhead)

## Shrine II

Shrine II is a retro FPS game with a focus on atmosphere and exploration. Explore a nightmarish world and battle great bosses where the surprises never end...

[Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1417010/) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/scumhead)

## Brutal Doom

Keep it as a gameplay-mod recommendation, not a separate campaign. Pair it only with maps and other mods whose compatibility is documented.

[ModDB download](https://www.moddb.com/mods/brutal-doom) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/sgtmarkiv)

## Castlevania: Simon's Destiny

A stylish adjacent pick, not a conventional gun shooter. Its handcrafted first-person reinterpretation makes it a worthwhile hidden discovery.

[itch.io download](https://batandy.itch.io/simonsdestiny) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/Batandy)

## Doom: The Golden Souls Remastered

A creative change of pace when you want secrets, jumping challenges, and whimsical worlds instead of another grim industrial corridor.

[itch.io download](https://batandy.itch.io/goldensouls) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/Batandy)

## Shrine

An approachable free introduction to Scumhead's horror work. The original Shrine belongs here even though its sequel has a Steam listing.

[itch.io download](https://scumhead.itch.io/shrine) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/scumhead)

## Doom: The Golden Souls 3 - Public Demo

Worth trying after the first two games. Keep expectations at demo scope; Patreon is an optional support route, not a claim of full-game availability.

[itch.io demo](https://batandy.itch.io/goldensouls3) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/Batandy)

## VietDoom - Public Alpha

An experimental deep cut, not a polished 2026 standalone release. The old public alpha and the creator's later standalone ambitions are different products.

[ModDB download](https://www.moddb.com/mods/vietdoom) · [Patreon support / devlogs](https://www.patreon.com/sgtmarkiv)
